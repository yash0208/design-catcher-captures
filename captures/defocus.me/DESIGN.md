## Overview

The defocus.me website presents a clean, minimalist aesthetic with a focus on clarity and subtle depth. The visual design is characterized by a sophisticated dark mode-like palette, leveraging shades of dark grey and black for text and surfaces, contrasted with crisp white and translucent elements. Typography plays a crucial role, pairing a classic serif for display headings with a modern system sans-serif for body text, creating a balanced and readable experience. Layouts are spacious and content-focused, utilizing generous whitespace and a grid-like structure to organize information. The overall impression is one of calm, focus, and understated elegance, reflecting the product's promise of reducing digital distraction.

**Key Characteristics:**

*   **Dark-leaning palette**: Predominantly uses dark greys, black, and white, with subtle accents.
*   **Translucent elements**: Glassmorphism-inspired navigation and card backgrounds create a sense of depth.
*   **Serif/Sans-serif pairing**: "Instrument Serif" for headlines, system sans-serif (SF Pro/Inter) for body.
*   **Generous whitespace**: Ample padding and margins create breathing room around content.
*   **Soft, subtle shadows**: Elevate elements without harshness, contributing to depth.
*   **Rounded corners**: Consistent use of various radii for a friendly, modern feel.
*   **Content-first layout**: Focuses on readability and clear presentation of information.
*   **Minimalist interaction**: Buttons are clear and direct, without excessive ornamentation.

## Typography

### Font Family
The primary typefaces are a system font stack, prioritizing Apple's "SF Pro Text" or "SF Pro Display" on macOS, "Segoe UI" on Windows, and "Inter" or other system-ui sans-serifs, for body text and UI elements. Display headings utilize the elegant serif typeface "Instrument Serif", with "Georgia" as a fallback.

*   **Primary Sans-serif**: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Text", "SF Pro Display", "Helvetica Neue", "Segoe UI Variable Text", "Segoe UI", Inter, system-ui, sans-serif`
*   **Display Serif**: `"Instrument Serif", Georgia, serif`
