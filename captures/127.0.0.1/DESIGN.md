## Overview

This digital product presents a modern, sophisticated aesthetic, primarily leveraging a stark contrast between a dark canvas and crisp white typography, punctuated by vibrant accent colors. The visual identity is clean and professional, suggesting a focus on technology, innovation, and clarity. Typography plays a central role, utilizing a strong display sans-serif for headlines and a monospace font for data points, creating a distinct and memorable voice. Layouts are spacious and structured, employing generous padding and clear sectioning to guide the user's eye. The overall impression is one of high-tech elegance and purposeful design.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Dark Mode First:** Predominantly dark background `{colors.canvas}` — #000000, with light text `{colors.on-canvas}` — #ffffff.
*   **Vibrant Accents:** A primary accent of `{colors.brand-primary}` — #f25325, with secondary accents like `{colors.brand-secondary}` — #c823ff and `{colors.brand-tertiary}` — #ff2323.
*   **Modern Typography:** "Inter Display" for headings and body, paired with "IBM Plex Mono" for numerical data.
*   **Spacious Layouts:** Generous vertical and horizontal padding, creating a sense of openness and hierarchy.
*   **Subtle Depth:** Minimal use of shadows, indicating a flat design philosophy with occasional emphasis.
*   **Rounded Elements:** Softly rounded corners on cards and interactive elements, balancing the sharp contrasts.
*   **Content-Focused:** Design choices prioritize readability and clear presentation of information.

## Colors

### Brand & Accent
*   **Brand Primary** (`{colors.brand-primary}` — #f25325): Used for prominent call-to-action backgrounds and key interactive elements.
*   **Brand Secondary** (`{colors.brand-secondary}` — #c823ff): A vibrant purple, used sparingly for highlights or secondary interactive states.
*   **Brand Tertiary** (`{colors.brand-tertiary}` — #ff2323): A bright red, used sparingly for highlights or specific interactive states.
*   **Button Primary Background** (`{colors.button-primary-bg}` — #ffb14e): A warm deep orange, used for primary button backgrounds.
*   **Button Primary Border** (`{colors.button-primary-border}` — #d68a59): A terracotta shade, used for primary button borders.

### Surface
*   **Canvas** (`{colors.canvas}` — #000000): The primary background color for the entire page, establishing a dark theme.
*   **Surface Dark** (`{colors.surface-dark}` — #141414): A slightly lighter shade of black, likely used for subtle differentiation of sections or components on the dark canvas.
*   **Surface Card** (`{colors.surface-card}` — #ffffff): Used for card backgrounds, providing a strong contrast against the dark canvas.

### Hairlines & Borders
*   **Border Light** (`{colors.border-light}` — #e5e7eb): A very light gray, used for subtle borders or dividers, often on light surfaces.
*   **Border Muted** (`{colors.border-muted}` — #a0a0a0): A soft gray, used for less prominent borders or separators.

### Text
*   **On Canvas** (`{colors.on-canvas}` — #ffffff): Primary text color used on the dark `{colors.canvas}` background.
*   **Text Primary Dark** (`{colors.text-primary-dark}` — #212121): Dark text color, likely used on light surfaces like `{colors.surface-card}`.
*   **Text Primary** (`{colors.text-primary}` — #333333): A dark gray, used for general body text on light backgrounds.
*   **Text Muted** (`{colors.text-muted}` — #a0a0a0): A soft gray, used for secondary or less emphasized text.

### Semantic
*   **Status Success Background** (`{colors.status-success-bg}` — #81c784): A soft green, used for success indicators.
*   **Status Success Text** (`{colors.status-success-text}` — #28702c): A strong green, used for text within success indicators.
*   **Status Error** (`{colors.status-error}` — #e57373): A soft red, used for error messages or indicators.
*   **Button Danger Background** (`{colors.button-danger-bg}` — #ffcccb): A very soft red, used for danger action buttons.
*   **Button Danger Text** (`{colors.button-danger-text}` — #7f0000): A very dark red, used for text on danger action buttons.
*   **Button Danger Border** (`{colors.button-danger-border}` — #b71c1c): A dark red, used for borders on danger action buttons.

## Typography

### Font Family
The primary typeface for display and body text is "Inter Display", with a fallback to "Inter Display Placeholder" and generic `sans-serif`. For numerical data and specific highlights, "IBM Plex Mono" provides a distinct monospace voice, falling back to generic `monospace`.
