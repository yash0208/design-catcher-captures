## Overview

The Revolve website presents a clean, modern, and professional aesthetic, emphasizing clarity and a strong brand identity centered around sustainability. The design leverages a limited color palette of dark greens, white, and a vibrant accent green to create a sophisticated yet approachable feel. Typography is primarily driven by the "BDO Grotesk" sans-serif typeface, offering excellent readability across various scales, from prominent headlines to detailed body text. Layouts are structured with generous whitespace, often employing full-width sections and centered content blocks, creating a sense of order and focus. The overall impression is one of efficiency and trustworthiness, aligning with the site's focus on supply chain transformation.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Color Palette**: Dominated by deep greens (`{colors.surface-dark}` — #272b27), crisp white (`{colors.canvas-light}` — #ffffff), and a bright, energetic green accent (`{colors.brand-primary}` — #56db56).
*   **Typography**: Exclusive use of "BDO Grotesk" for all text, featuring varied weights and sizes to establish a clear content hierarchy.
*   **Layout**: Section-based, often full-width, with content contained within consistent horizontal padding. Generous vertical spacing between sections.
*   **Whitespace**: Abundant use of whitespace around content blocks and text, contributing to a clean and uncluttered appearance.
*   **Minimalist Depth**: Subtle shadows (`{shadow.sm}` — rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 3px 6px 0px) are used sparingly to provide gentle elevation rather than strong depth.
*   **Subtle Rounding**: Components feature slight corner rounding (`{rounded.sm}` — 6px) for a softer, modern touch, contrasting with sharp edges.
*   **Content-focused**: Design choices prioritize readability and clear presentation of information, avoiding overly decorative elements.
*   **Brand Alignment**: The green color scheme strongly reinforces the "eco-supply pipeline" and sustainability messaging.

## Colors

*   **Brand & Accent**
    *   **Primary** (`{colors.brand-primary}` — #56db56): Used for call-to-action buttons, key highlights, and elements signifying positive status or action.
    *   **Accent Orange** (`{colors.accent-orange}` — #ea991f): An secondary accent color, used sparingly for specific highlights.
*   **Surface**
    *   **Canvas Light** (`{colors.canvas-light}` — #ffffff): The primary background color for most content sections and elements, providing a clean base.
    *   **Canvas Light Alt** (`{colors.canvas-light-alt}` — #f7fdf7): A very light off-white, used as the body background and for subtle section differentiation.
    *   **Surface Light Alt** (`{colors.surface-light-alt}` — #e7f0e7): A light, muted green used for background sections, offering a gentle contrast to pure white.
    *   **Surface Dark** (`{colors.surface-dark}` — #272b27): Used for darker background sections, especially in footers or hero areas, to create visual separation and contrast.
    *   **Surface Dark Alt** (`{colors.surface-dark-alt}` — #384438): A slightly lighter dark green, used for specific dark background elements.
    *   **Surface Light Subtle** (`{colors.surface-light-subtle}` — #fafafa): A very light gray, likely for subtle background variations or hover states.
*   **Hairlines & Borders**
    *   **Border Dark Subtle** (`{colors.border-dark-subtle}` — #272b27): Used for subtle borders or outlines on dark text elements.
    *   **Border Light** (`{colors.border-light}` — #ffffff): Used for borders or outlines on elements against dark backgrounds.
    *   **Border Muted** (`{colors.border-muted}` — #aaadb0): Used for subtle borders, dividers, or inactive states.
    *   **Border Black** (`{colors.border-black}` — #000000): Used for strong borders or outlines, often with transparency for subtle effect (e.g., `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`).
*   **Text**
    *   **Text Primary** (`{colors.text-primary}` — #272b27): The main text color for body copy and headings on light backgrounds, ensuring high readability.
    *   **Text On Dark** (`{colors.text-on-dark}` — #ffffff): Used for text placed on dark backgrounds, providing strong contrast.
    *   **Text Muted** (`{colors.text-muted}` — #aaadb0): Used for secondary information, captions, or less emphasized text.

## Typography

### Font Family
The primary typeface for the entire site is **"BDO Grotesk", sans-serif**. This modern sans-serif is used consistently across all text elements, from headings to body copy, ensuring a unified and professional appearance. A secondary font, "Inter var", sans-serif, appears in the extraction but is used minimally, suggesting it might be a fallback or used in a very specific, isolated context.
