## Recommended Stack

*   **shadcn/ui**: For foundational UI components such as navigation menus, buttons, cards, and form inputs, providing accessibility and a solid styling base.
*   **Tailwind CSS**: As the styling framework, leveraging its utility-first approach for rapid development and consistent design, especially when combined with shadcn/ui.
*   **Framer Motion**: To implement subtle entrance animations like the detected fade-in effect for specific elements, offering a declarative way to handle animations in React.
