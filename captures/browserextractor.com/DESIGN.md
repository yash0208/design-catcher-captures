## Overview

The Browser Extractor website presents a modern, sophisticated, and technically oriented visual identity, primarily built on a dark theme. Its aesthetic is characterized by a blend of clean typography, subtle glassmorphism, and strategic use of a vibrant orange accent. The overall impression is one of precision and clarity, reflecting the tool's function. The design effectively balances a professional feel with engaging visual elements, using a structured layout to guide the user through complex information.

The brand signals a tool that is powerful yet approachable, with a focus on detailed inspection and intuitive interaction. Typography plays a crucial role, with a strong contrast between the modern, geometric "Space Grotesk" for headlines and the highly legible "Avenir Next" for body content, occasionally complemented by the classic "Iowan Old Style" for specific stylistic emphasis. The consistent use of a dark canvas with bright, accessible text ensures readability, while the primary accent color provides clear calls to action and highlights key features.

**Key Characteristics**
*   **Dark-Themed Canvas**: Predominantly uses `{colors.canvas.dark}` — #121212 as the base, creating a high-contrast environment.
*   **Geometric Headline Typography**: Features "Space Grotesk" for bold, modern headings.
*   **Legible Body Typography**: Utilizes "Avenir Next" for clear and comfortable reading of longer texts.
*   **Vibrant Orange Accent**: `{colors.brand.primary}` — #ffab5c is used for CTAs and highlights.
*   **Subtle Glassmorphism**: Elements like the header and cards incorporate transparent backgrounds and blurred effects.
*   **Structured Layout**: Employs a consistent max-width container `{layout.content-max-width}` — 1320px and clear vertical spacing.
*   **Monospaced Code Elements**: "JetBrains Mono" is used for code snippets and technical details.
*   **Minimalist Iconography**: UI elements are clean and functional, avoiding visual clutter.

## Typography

### Font Family
The primary typefaces used across the site are "Space Grotesk" for display and prominent headings, and "Avenir Next" for body text and general UI elements. "JetBrains Mono" is employed for code snippets and technical information, providing a clear monospaced presentation. A classic serif, "Iowan Old Style", is used sparingly for specific stylistic headings, adding a touch of elegance.

*   **Display & Headings**: "Space Grotesk", system-ui, sans-serif
*   **Body & UI**: "Avenir Next", Avenir, "Segoe UI", system-ui, sans-serif
*   **Code & Technical**: "JetBrains Mono", ui-monospace, monospace
*   **Stylistic Headings**: "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Palatino, Georgia, serif
