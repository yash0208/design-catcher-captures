## Overview

The Creem website presents a modern, sophisticated, and slightly edgy aesthetic, primarily leveraging a dark mode interface with vibrant accent colors. The visual identity is characterized by a strong contrast between deep, near-black surfaces and crisp, light typography, punctuated by bold, geometric display headings. The overall impression is one of a high-tech, professional platform with a playful, creative undertone. Generous spacing and a clear typographic hierarchy contribute to excellent readability and a premium feel.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Dark-first Theme**: Predominantly dark backgrounds (`{colors.surface-primary}` — #121314, `{colors.surface-brand-dark}` — #151617) with light text (`{colors.text-on-dark}` — #fafaf9).
*   **Typographic Contrast**: A striking pairing of a bold, condensed display typeface ("gasoekOne") for headlines and a clean, modern sans-serif ("GeistSans") for body text.
*   **Vibrant Accents**: Strategic use of bright peach (`{colors.accent-peach}` — #ffbe98), purple (`{colors.accent-purple}` — #b09cfb), and green (`{colors.accent-green}` — #4ecb71) for calls to action and highlights.
*   **Subtle Depth**: Minimal shadows, primarily used as a strong, offset outline (`{shadow.md}` — rgb(21, 22, 23) 3px 3px 0px 0px) rather than traditional drop shadows, adding a graphic, almost sticker-like quality.
*   **Rounded Rectangles**: Consistent use of moderately rounded corners (`{rounded.md}` — 12px) on interactive elements and content blocks.
*   **Spacious Layout**: Ample whitespace and a clear grid structure ensure content is easily digestible and visually appealing.
*   **Dynamic Headlines**: Large, responsive display typography that scales dramatically across breakpoints.

## Typography

### Font Family
The primary sans-serif typeface for body text and UI elements is "GeistSans", with "GeistSans Fallback" as its immediate fallback. For display headings and prominent titles, a distinctive, condensed sans-serif typeface named "gasoekOne" (with "gasoekOne Fallback", "Gasoek One", system-ui, sans-serif) is used to create a strong visual impact.
