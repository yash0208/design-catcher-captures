## Overview

The Freehand website presents a professional and modern aesthetic, balancing a clean, spacious layout with strategic use of brand colors to highlight key information and calls to action. The visual identity is characterized by a strong typographic hierarchy, employing a sophisticated serif for display text and a highly legible sans-serif for body content, complemented by a monospace font for functional elements. The overall impression is one of clarity, trustworthiness, and innovation, reflecting the company's focus on AI solutions for global trade.

The design leverages ample whitespace, particularly around large headings and content blocks, to create a sense of calm and focus. A vibrant orange serves as the primary brand accent, drawing attention to interactive elements and important statistics. Subtle background textures and light, earthy tones provide a warm counterpoint to the otherwise stark black and white palette.

**Key Characteristics:**
*   **Typographic Contrast**: "BL Pylon Serif" for headlines, "Fustat" for body, "Geist Mono" for labels.
*   **Brand Accent**: Prominent use of `{colors.brand-primary}` — #fe6200 for CTAs and highlights.
*   **Spacious Layout**: Generous `{spacing.section-gap-xxl}` — 80px and `{spacing.container-padding}` — 61px create an airy feel.
*   **Neutral Surfaces**: Predominantly uses `{colors.canvas}` — #ffffff and `{colors.canvas-default}` — #faf3e8 for backgrounds.
*   **Subtle Depth**: Minimal use of shadows, relying on color and spacing for hierarchy.
*   **Functional Monospace**: `{typography.label-md}` — Geist Mono for buttons and specific callouts.
*   **Clear Information Architecture**: Headings and body text are clearly differentiated by size, weight, and font family.
*   **Marquee Elements**: Dynamic, continuously scrolling sections for logos and content.
